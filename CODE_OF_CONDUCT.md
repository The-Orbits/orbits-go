# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space it uses, is expected
to be respectful, patient and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory or exclusionary language
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may block people who keep breaking them.
Report a problem privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/The-Orbits/orbits-go/security/advisories/new) form (it reaches the
maintainers only), noting that it is a conduct report.
